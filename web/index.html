More free edits

The AI runs on free Hugging Face servers. Without an account each person gets about 2 minutes of AI time a day; with a free Hugging Face account it's about 5 minutes. Simple edits (brightness, contrast, black and white, rotate, flip, crop) run on your device and are unlimited.

To use your own account's allowance, create a free token at huggingface.co → Settings → Access Tokens (type “Read”) and paste it here. It stays in this tab only and is sent only to Hugging Face.

How it works and privacy

Photo edits use FLUX.2 [klein] 4B and video edits use Kiwi-Edit, both open models, running on public Hugging Face Spaces operated by the model makers. When you ask for an AI edit, your files are sent to those Spaces. Simple edits never leave your device. Nothing is stored by this site.

Videos: up to 5 seconds works best. Each video edit uses about 4 minutes of AI time, so it needs a free Hugging Face token and uses most of a day's free allowance.

Reframe AI is free and open source. Edits are made by AI and can be wrong — label edited images honestly.
